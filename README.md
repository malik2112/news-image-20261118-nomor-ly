Built for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 18 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118).

A front page summing up the world news of **Wednesday, 18 November 2026** in
one image, led by the day's biggest story — the US–Iran strikes in the Gulf —
with four more stories below it and three in the bottom strip.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) opens with a wide view of the Gulf at dusk:
US jets and a warship as missiles rise from Qeshm Island. Below it:

| Panel | Story |
| --- | --- |
| Bridge down | Ukraine hits the Kerch bridge again; a span falls into the strait |
| Shenzhen | Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet at APEC before three-way talks with Donald Trump |
| Alaska votes | Mary Peltola takes the Senate seat; Democrats lead 56–44 |
| Voyager 1 | First probe to reach one light-day from Earth |

Bottom strip: Ebola in Congo passes 11,000 cases; the US loses its
measles-free status after 26 years; a Russian military plane crashes in
Siberia, killing all 19 aboard.

The artwork was generated as text-free editorial illustrations; the masthead,
headlines and captions were typeset over them so every word is crisp and
legible. Sources checked on 18 November 2026: Zark News daily briefing,
One Turn News, Horizon world news for 18 November 2026, The Munich Eye /
The Paris Eye breaking news pages, and the Medium strategic news brief for
18 November 2026.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`). It runs with
no network and writes identical bytes on every run.
